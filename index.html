How this app uses your location

  • When you press Record entry, the app reads your phone's GPS once and sends it to GPI.
  • GPI uses it only to check whether you are inside the factory (GPI, Paras or Zahir).
  • The app does not track you in the background. You must open it and record an entry in every entry window.
  • GPI administrators can see your entries: time, location, GPS accuracy and result.
